# 🎬 Creative Vision — Signal Lost

> **Pillar 1:** Dread is in what the scanner reveals — and what it summons.
> **Pillar 2:** The story is the signal. The signal is a person.
> **Pillar 3:** Every scan is a choice. Every choice is risk.

## Player Fantasy

You are *the technician who answered the call*. Routine maintenance. Solo posting. A relay station floating between two stars. Then your scanner picks up a frequency that shouldn't exist — and a voice you almost recognise. You are not a soldier. You have no weapon. You have a scanner, a flashlight, three respawns at the Safe Rooms, and a creeping certainty that the last operator never actually left.

## Tone Reference

- *Iron Lung* (Markiplier-era dread, claustrophobia, audio-driven)
- *Mouthwashing* (ugliness, intimacy, no easy monsters)
- *Crow Country* (PS1 aesthetic, scan-the-environment loop)
- *Soma* (operator's last log = your survival manual)

## Visual Direction

- **Low-poly + low-fi:** Synty POLYGON Sci-Fi Space. Posterised lighting. Heavy URP post-FX: vignette, film grain, CRT scanlines on UI overlays.
- **Lighting:** mostly *dark*. Player-carried light + scanner pings are the only readable signals.
- **Palette:** Cold blues and greys for the station. Sickly green for scan UI. Red only appears when the Carrier is in line of sight.

## Audio Direction

- Diegetic-first. Footsteps, scanner pings, hull groans, distant atmospheric processors.
- Carrier has no theme music — only *signal interference* as a proximity tell.
- Audio logs delivered by a single voice actor; written to be heartbreaking, not exposition-dumpy.

## Non-Goals (explicit)

- ❌ Combat. No guns, no melee. You cannot fight.
- ❌ Jump scares as primary tool. Dread > startle.
- ❌ Inventory micromanagement. One scanner, one flashlight.
- ❌ Open-world. Hand-crafted, linear-ish, dense.
